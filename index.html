The AI That Actually Does the Work

Most AI tools tell you what to do. Cowork does it. It's a digital coworker that lives on your desktop, connects to your files and apps, and completes multi-step tasks from start to finish — while you're doing something else.

Built by Anthropic (the same company behind Claude), Cowork is not a chatbot. It's an autonomous AI agent that lives on your desktop. You give it a task — it goes and handles it. It reads your files, writes new ones, organizes folders, generates formatted reports, saves drafts to your Google Drive, checks your calendar, and more. All without you coordinating a single step.

You describe the outcome. Cowork figures out the plan and executes it. You can step away, drive to a job site, take a call — and come back to finished work.

No coding. No technical setup. No new workflows to learn. Just tell it what you need in plain English. It connects to Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar out of the box — tools you're already using.
